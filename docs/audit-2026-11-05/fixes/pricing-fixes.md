## The model (ddc170e, `Audit: PRC-01 (round 3, H1 and H2)`)
A comp is "these items are free", a property of the items. The comp line now carries `comped: { addons: [catalog ids], bar: boolean, syrups: [flavor ids] }`, read at comp time off the waived invoice's own line items (an addon line's `source_id` is `service_addons.id`; a bar line is the one the drink plan added; a syrup line carries no ids, so the flavors are read off the newest drink plan the way `writeExtrasLineItems` charged them, `syrupSelections` less `syrupSelfProvided`, kept to the flavors the booking holds). ONE pure rule, `compLine.js applyCompLines(lines, priced)`, sets every comp line to the current price of its comped items that are present, off the engine's own output: an add-on's `line_total`; a bar at its marginal fee in the current bar count (the first-bar fee when it is the only bar, the additional fee above it; several comped bars take the top of the count one after another, so two comps never claim one fee twice and together never more than every bar); the comped flavors at their marginal price in the current selection (the set's price less the set's price without them, since the engine prices flavors as a set with the 3-pack discount; that marginal is exactly what the fold added for them). An absent item contributes nothing; a line whose items are all absent is dropped; the stored amount is refreshed, so the column and the snapshot show the current comp. `calculateProposalWithComps(args)` wraps the engine (one pass for the items, a second with the refreshed lines when any moved) and is what the five pricers call: the fold's After leg (cancel-line, the lab save, the submit; the Before leg keeps the stored line), `computeCatalogSnapshot`, the editor PATCH, `/calculate`, `priceProposedState` (options quote, switch, change request). The fold keeps writing the column when a line moved. `netCompLinesAgainstAddons` and the `comped_addons` cents split are gone, with the round-2 tests that pinned frozen cents (said in the commit). The writer derives the cents at comp time from the row's snapshot when it carries the item shapes, refuses one that carries them but prices none of the comped items (`COMP_ITEMS_NOT_PRICED`, the invoice is then a correction), and takes the invoice's folded cents on a legacy `{}` row.
