# Payments audit (money in, row truth, refunds, reminders) — Dr. Bartender OS @ 13f2645, 2026-11-05

Summary
- Findings: 2 high, 2 medium, 5 low (9 total); 7 CONFIRMED by end-to-end code trace, 2 PLAUSIBLE (depend on database state).
- Worst: PAY-01 — a syrup-only "Drink Plan Extras" payment is rolled into `amount_paid` but never into `total_price`, so every balance the system charges or bills afterwards (autopay, admin charge-balance, the refreshed Balance invoice, the drink-plan balance fold, the client-facing balance) is short by the syrup money; the owner's ledger records this only as a display shortfall on the Events lists.
- Second: PAY-02 — the send-time Deposit invoice is payable through the public invoice page before the agreement is signed (the client portal itself renders "Pay balance" on an unbooked proposal), and the webhook's invoice branch credits the money without converting the booking (status stays `sent`/`viewed`/`accepted`, no Balance invoice, no reminder ladder, no autopay), while a shift is still created and a second deposit can be taken later.
- Posture: the money core is in good shape. Webhook signature/raw body, the test-secret gate, idempotency (partial unique index on succeeded `proposal_payments`, `webhook_events` for the keyless events), the gratuity SAVEPOINT, refund scope under the row lock, the pending-row-before-Stripe refund protocol and its sweep, the cancellation math in cents, the autopay atomic claim + Stripe idempotency key + durable intent record + prior-intent guard, and the bank-debit in-flight guard all held up. The client never computes money the server trusts, and the error funnel leaks nothing beyond the allowed admin-route decline reason.
- The defects cluster at two seams: money that is collected but is not contract money (PAY-01, PAY-04), and contract invoices reachable outside the sign-and-pay rail (PAY-02). Four ledger items were re-verified as still present (listed after the findings) and are not re-reported.
