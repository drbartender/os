Menu Designer

The admin page that turns a client's drink plan into a framed 8×10 bar menu, and the layout system that menu sits on. The admin chrome is a stand-in for the OS skin, so swap in its real tokens. The art is placeholder line work; the AI paints the real backgrounds and drinks.

Part 2 · Layout rules

800 × 1000 canvas · 100 px = 1 in

One system, not 64 templates. Rows hold up to four drinks and split top-heavy. Text is laid out first; the art takes whatever height is left.

  • RowsUp to 4 per row. 5 → 3 + 2, 7 → 4 + 3. Short rows center under long ones.
  • MocktailsStart a new row under their own heading, split by the same rule. They count toward the 8 illustrated.
  • Art sizeStarts large and shrinks in 6 px steps until the page fits. It never goes below 0.65 in (65 px). Text never shrinks for art.
  • LogoAbove the title adds about 1 in at the top. Replacing the title takes the title's space at 1.5 in tall.
  • More from the barDrinks 9–12 sit between the drinks and Also at the bar, in two text columns.
  • Also at the barPinned to the bottom, above the mark row. Leaving it out gives its space back to the art.
  • Safe areaText, logo and mark stay 0.4 in (40 px) inside the trim. The background runs to the edge.
  • Type floorsDescriptions 10 pt (14 px). Names 14 pt (20 px). Overflow steps down once, then flags "Too long".

Boards A–F

placeholder art

Finished boards from the brief. Each one uses a different pairing and backdrop, to show the same skeleton holding up under very different art.

Legibility over unknown art

board C × 4 backdrops

Three defenses stack up. The background prompt asks for a calm center. A text backing sits behind the words. The ink set flips between each pairing's light-art and dark-art colors, based on how bright the art behind the text is.

Text backing
Off

Bare text. Only holds when the center really is calm, like the garden here. On the speakeasy, the cobwebs cut into the title. Dallas has to turn it on deliberately.

Soft (default)

A feathered scrim in the art's own ground color, strongest behind the text column and gone by the edges. The art still reads. Its limit: high-contrast detail in the center still shows through at about 20%.

Panel

A frosted card with a hairline in the accent color. Always legible, but it hides the most art. Use it when the art comes back busy or when a photo-like style reference was used.

Font pairings

Google Fonts · title / heading / body

Six pairings, each with default ink and accent colors for light art and for dark art. The AI pre-picks one by theme, and Dallas can override both colors.
