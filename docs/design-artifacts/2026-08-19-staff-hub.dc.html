The Staff hub · one entry, four children

Two levels, two vocabularies. Hub sections are underline tabs fused to the page header (new .hub-tabs). Views inside a child stay .seg pills in the toolbar. The eye reads "part of the title" vs "a control in the content", so the two levels never look like the same strip twice. A third treatment is never needed, which retires legacy .tab-nav/.tab-btn and the hand-rolled underline buttons in Tips and Reviews.

Q1, shared chrome: the hub carries one live subtitle line (roster count, open pay run, decisions waiting) and nothing else. No KPI band; the child page is the page. Q2, Payroll's four tabs: they stay a .seg inside Payroll. Promoting them makes seven siblings of wildly unequal weight, and the two-vocabulary rule already makes the nesting legible. Q3, Reviews at one row: it earns its tab (cross-staff, writes real payroll lines) but drops internal tabs entirely: pending reviews are workbench cards, resolved ones are table rows, the contest is a side rail. Nothing to relabel when volume arrives.

Badge math: the sidebar Staff badge is the sum of decisions waiting across children (1 new application + 1 pending review = 2). One click in, the hub tabs decompose it per child. Old routes /hiring, /tips, /reviews, /financials/payroll become param-preserving redirects; the Overview deep link ?tab=payrun&period=<id> keeps working.

1jThe hub pattern · spec for reuseclient/src/index.css · Marketing adopts next
Two levels, two vocabularies
The reusable rule for every hub: Staff now, Marketing next.

Level 1 · hub sections

.hub-tabs
Roster 18 Hiring 1 Payroll Reviews 1
Underline tabs fused to the page header. Routes, not state: each tab is a URL. Counts in mono (.hub-tab-count) are ambient; badges (.hub-tab-badge) mean a decision is waiting. One .hub-tabs per page, ever.

Level 2 · views of one surface

.seg (existing)
The Toolbar's segmented pill, unchanged, in the content area. It filters or re-lenses what is already on screen. URL-backed via ?tab= as today. Never render two .segs stacked; the second one is a select or a toolbar filter.

The law

RuleDetail
Which level am I onHeader-fused underline = where you are. Pill in the toolbar = what you are looking at. No third level exists: a child needing one restructures (Reviews became card + table + rail).
Badge mathSidebar badge on Staff = sum of decisions waiting across children. Hub tab badges decompose it per child. Counts are not badges: Roster 18 is ambient, mono, gray.
Hub chromeOne shared title + one live subtitle line (roster count, open pay run, decisions). No KPI band above the tabs; the child page owns its stats.
ConsolidationRetired: legacy .tab-nav/.tab-btn (amber tokens) and the hand-rolled underline buttons in TipsAdmin and StaffReviews. New surfaces choose .hub-tabs or .seg, nothing else.
Routes/staffing lands on Roster. /hiring, /tips, /reviews, /financials/payroll redirect preserving params. Overview's ?tab=payrun&period=<id> deep link keeps working.
Responsive.hub-tabs scrolls horizontally under 720px, no wrapping. Wide tables scroll in .tbl-wrap, never the page. Both skins flip on [data-app="admin-os"][data-skin].
Marketing adoption.mkt-tabrow's .seg becomes .hub-tabs one for one; the budget meter moves into the live subtitle line. .seg stays for its internal filters.
